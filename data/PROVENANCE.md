# Synthetic material provenance

The three cases in `supportbench/cases.py`, the GroveDesk lamp and PebbleFan
product descriptions, their fictional references (`DEMO-*`), case records,
customer prompts, and short support guides were authored for this repository.
The environment, scorer, scripted policies, tests, and episode diagram are
original MIT-licensed work. The project uses no private logs, real customer
records, proprietary company playbooks, or copied external CRM data.

Research sources are cited in [docs/research.md](../docs/research.md) for
context, not included as code or training data. New contributed cases should
carry their own source and licensing notes; prefer invented, low-stakes
scenarios without real contact details or stereotypes.
